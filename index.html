Trained, experienced midwives

Every group, class and drop-in is led by qualified midwives, so the advice you get is grounded in real professional training and experience.

Expertise without the clinic

Ask about feeding, recovery, your birth or those 3am worries over a coffee, in a relaxed space with no appointment and no waiting room.

Built for local families, from the first positive test through the early years, so you can find your village close to home.

A little village under one roof

Somewhere to come throughout pregnancy and the early years, make friends, ask questions and feel supported rather than isolated.

The café
